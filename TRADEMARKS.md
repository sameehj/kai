# Trademarks

"Kai" and the Kai logo are trademarks of the project's maintainer (placeholder —
set the legal entity before launch).

The GPLv3 covers the **code**. It does not grant rights to the **name** or
**logo**. This is standard practice (Mozilla, Rust, and many others separate
code licenses from trademark use) and it protects users: it stops a malicious
fork from shipping under the "Kai" name and reputation.

**You may**, without asking:

- Use the name "Kai" to refer to this project.
- State that your product "works with Kai" or "is built on Kai", accurately.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies you redistribute under the GPLv3.

**Please do not**, without written permission:

- Ship a **modified** version under the name "Kai" or a confusingly similar
  name, or use the logo for it. Rename your fork.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the project endorses your product.

> **To do before launch:** run a trademark search ("Kai" is a common word and
> name) and confirm the mark is available in your jurisdiction and class. If it
> is taken, pick another name early — renaming later is costly.
