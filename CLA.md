# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Note:** This is a plain-language draft for review. Have a lawyer review and
> finalize it before you accept outside contributions or rely on it to sell
> commercial licenses. It is modeled on the widely used Apache Individual CLA.

Thank you for contributing to Kai. So that the project can stay open source
**and** offer commercial licenses (which fund the work), we ask every
contributor to agree to the following before we merge their contribution.

## 1. You keep your copyright

You keep full ownership of your contribution. This agreement does not transfer
your copyright. It only grants licenses.

## 2. Copyright license

You grant the project maintainer (and recipients of software the maintainer
distributes) a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display,
sublicense, and distribute your contribution and derivative works.

**This license is broad enough that the maintainer may include your
contribution in software distributed under the GPLv3 *and* under separate
commercial terms.** That dual distribution is the point of this agreement.

## 3. Patent license

You grant a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
patent license to make, use, sell, and distribute your contribution, for any
patent claims you own that your contribution necessarily infringes.

## 4. You are allowed to grant this

You confirm that each contribution is your original work, or that you have the
right to submit it under this agreement, and that it does not knowingly violate
anyone else's rights. If your employer has rights to work you create, you
confirm you have permission to contribute, or that your employer has waived
those rights for contributions to Kai.

## 5. No obligation

You understand the maintainer is not required to use your contribution.

## 6. No warranty

Contributions are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

---

**How we collect it:** a CLA-assistant bot comments on your first pull request
with a one-click sign-off that records your agreement against your GitHub
identity. You only sign once.

Third-party code (code you did not write) may only be added under a permissive
license (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0) and must be recorded in `THIRD_PARTY.md`. The CLA
does not apply to that code; its own license does.
